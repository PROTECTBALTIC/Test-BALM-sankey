How is the summed pathway indicator calculated?

The diagram combines the scored relationships along each complete pathway. It calculates a score for each species → trait category → ecological function → ecosystem service combination, then adds those scores together.

Individual pathway score
Species–trait value × (trait–function score ÷ 3) × (function–service score ÷ 3)

Dividing each 0–3 relationship score by 3 expresses it on a 0–1 scale. This is a modelling choice in this explorer, not a claim about the officially prescribed BALM calculation.

Select at least one species and one ecosystem service to see a worked example using the source matrices.

Summed pathway indicator = the sum of all individual pathway scores currently displayed. When several species or services are selected, their pathways are added together. In Simplified view, only the 10 highest-scoring individual pathways are included in the displayed sum.

The indicator has no physical unit. It is not a measurement of carbon stored, service supply or species abundance, and the aggregation has not been validated as the official BALM calculation.

Calculation: For each recorded trait category, the species trait value is multiplied by the trait–function score (0–3) and function–service score (0–3), each divided by 3. Pathways are then summed for display. In simplified view, only the ten highest-scoring individual species–trait–function–service pathways are included; hidden pathways are excluded from the displayed totals. This is a transparent exploratory aggregation, not a verified reproduction of BALM's official calculation procedure. The score is dimensionless; it does not measure carbon sequestration, species abundance or service supply.
