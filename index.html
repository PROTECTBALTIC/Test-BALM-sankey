Calculation: For each recorded trait category, the species trait value is multiplied by the trait–function score (0–3) and function–service score (0–3), each divided by 3. Pathways are then summed for display. This is a transparent exploratory aggregation, not a verified reproduction of BALM's official calculation procedure. The score is dimensionless; it does not measure carbon sequestration, species abundance or service supply. Source: PROTECT BALTIC D4.1 trait and TxF/FxES matrices (2026).
